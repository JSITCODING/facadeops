# FacadeOps 30-day validation plan

## Week 1 — Evidence and recruitment

- Finalize research consent, interview notes template, synthetic report, and concept-site preview.
- Build a candidate list of property managers across condominium, office, hospitality, retail, and mixed-use buildings.
- Recruit through direct introductions and professional contacts; do not scrape private contact data.
- Book the first five property-manager interviews and two specialist interviews.
- Ask two drone operators and two technical reviewers for scope/partner conversations without implying selection.

**Exit:** Five interviews scheduled, research data structure ready, no unsupported public claims.

## Week 2 — Problem interviews

- Complete at least six property-manager interviews.
- Collect redacted examples of current reports or workflows where participants voluntarily provide them.
- Code evidence by trigger, current method, decision owner, friction, retained record, frequency, and willingness to take a next step.
- Do not redesign the offer after every interview; document patterns and contradictions first.

**Exit:** At least four concrete recent examples and a provisional pattern summary.

## Week 3 — Partner and service feasibility

- Complete remaining manager interviews and specialist interviews.
- Verify the authorization path with candidate operators for two synthetic building scenarios.
- Review the sample record with a qualified technical professional.
- Obtain non-binding cost inputs for capture, technical review, insurance, transport, site control, and reporting.
- Update the pricing workbook without presenting estimates as market prices.

**Exit:** One viable operator path, one viable technical-review path, and a documented list of unresolved legal/insurance items.

## Week 4 — Offer test and decision

- Present the revised synthetic record and service scope to interview participants.
- Ask for a concrete next step: proposal request, site assessment, introduction to the approver, or explicit rejection.
- Do not conduct a flight or collect property imagery until every pilot gate is complete.
- Publish only aggregated, anonymized research and a transparent portfolio update.

**Continue threshold:** Six of ten managers confirm the problem, three request a proposal or site assessment, and accountable partners can deliver a compliant limited pilot.

**Pause threshold:** Interest is polite but no concrete next steps occur, existing providers already solve the record problem adequately, or the partner/insurance path makes the initial offer uneconomic.

## Final decision record

Document the sample, evidence, counter-evidence, risks, cost range, partner readiness, privacy model, and the chosen outcome: proceed to a narrowly scoped pilot, revise the target segment, retain as a portfolio research project, or stop.
