# FacadeOps market landscape

Research date: 27 September 2026. This is a discovery snapshot, not a complete competitor census.

## Current alternatives

| Alternative | What the customer gets | Strength | Likely gap to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reactive contractor visit | Quote and proposed work after a visible problem | Direct path to intervention | Evidence may be shaped by the contractor's scope; history may remain fragmented |
| Rope access, lift, or scaffold inspection | Close physical access and possible tactile assessment | Can support observations imagery alone cannot | Mobilization, site control, access, and disruption may be substantial |
| Standalone drone operator | Aerial images, video, survey, or inspection capture | Efficient access to selected viewpoints | Technical interpretation and recurring property record may be separate services |
| Facility-management provider | Broad building operations and maintenance coordination | Existing customer relationship and operational reach | Exterior evidence may be one small part of a broader contract |
| Drone facade washing provider | Cleaning with before/after documentation | Clear intervention and visible result | Cleaning evidence is not necessarily an independent condition assessment |
| Property-inspection software | Structured imagery and portfolio records | Repeatable documentation and permissions | Local capture, regulatory coordination, and technical review still require service partners |

## Discovered Angola examples

- ENTERIS advertises drone inspection and drone facade washing in Angola, including documented completed work. This invalidates any “first in Angola” or “no one does this” positioning.
- GeomatPro advertises aerial drone inspection and geospatial data services from Angola.
- ProspaceFM, ProCondo, Sokipa, AFM, and other firms address property or facilities management in Luanda. They may be customers, channels, partners, or competitors depending on the offer.

## Defensible positioning to validate

FacadeOps is not “a drone company.” Its proposed value is the coordination layer that turns a defined building question into controlled evidence, qualified interpretation, prioritized action, and a dated record that remains useful across contractors and inspection cycles.

This positioning is still an assumption. Interviews must establish whether managers value an independent recurring record, whether existing providers already deliver it adequately, and who is willing to pay for coordination separate from capture or repair.

## Questions the market scan does not answer

- How frequently Luanda property managers commission exterior inspection without a known defect.
- Whether managers prefer a standalone record provider or an integrated facility-management contract.
- Which professional qualifications customers expect for different facade materials and findings.
- What price separates a useful preventive service from an unnecessary overhead.
- Whether an authorized operator can economically serve the intended building types and locations.
