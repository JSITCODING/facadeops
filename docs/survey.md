# FacadeOps bilingual validation survey

The survey is anonymous. Contact details belong in a separate optional form. Do not ask for building address, resident details, access procedures, or identifiable photographs.

## Introduction / Introdução

**EN:** FacadeOps is a validation-stage concept exploring how property managers in Luanda inspect and document difficult-to-access building exteriors. This survey takes about four minutes. It does not advertise an operating, licensed, or certified service. Responses will be analyzed in aggregate.

**PT:** A FacadeOps é um conceito em fase de validação que estuda como gestores de imóveis em Luanda inspeccionam e documentam exteriores de edifícios de difícil acesso. O questionário demora cerca de quatro minutos. Não anuncia um serviço em operação, licenciado ou certificado. As respostas serão analisadas de forma agregada.

## Questions / Perguntas

1. **Role / Função** — Property manager; facilities manager; condominium administrator; building owner; maintenance contractor; other.
2. **Building responsibility / Responsabilidade** — Residential; office; hospitality; retail; mixed use; industrial; other. Select all that apply.
3. **Exterior inspection frequency / Frequência** — Never; only after a complaint or incident; annually; every 6–12 months; more frequently; unsure.
4. **Last trigger / Último motivo** — Visible deterioration; water entry; planned cleaning/painting; insurance; handover; complaint; preventive review; other.
5. **Methods used / Métodos utilizados** — Ground observation; ladder/lift/scaffold; rope access; drone imagery; contractor photos; no inspection; other.
6. **Current record / Registo actual** — Formal report; shared drive; email/WhatsApp; maintenance software; paper; photos without structure; no retained record.
7. **Main difficulties / Principais dificuldades** — Access; authorization; safety planning; cost; scheduling; disruption; finding qualified people; interpreting evidence; tracking history; approving a budget; other. Select up to three.
8. **Decision delay / Atraso na decisão** — In the most recent case, how long passed between recognizing the need and receiving information sufficient to decide? Same day; under one week; 1–4 weeks; over one month; never received sufficient information; unsure.
9. **Usefulness / Utilidade** — Rate the usefulness of one dated record containing annotated evidence, limitations, recommended next actions, and earlier inspection history. 1–5.
10. **Buying process / Compra** — Who would approve this work? Property manager; condominium board; building owner; procurement; facilities director; other; unsure.
11. **Next step / Próximo passo** — Would you review a synthetic sample report? Yes; maybe; no.
12. **Open question / Pergunta aberta** — What would FacadeOps need to understand before this approach could work for your building?

## Separate contact form

Collect only preferred name, organization, role, email/phone, preferred language, consent to be contacted, and whether the person is willing to review a sample record or discuss a site assessment. Set a retention period and delete non-responsive contact data after it expires.

## Analysis rules

- Report counts and percentages by participant type and building category.
- Do not publish cross-tabs that could identify an individual or property.
- Treat self-reported cost and delay as unverified until supported by an artifact or additional interview.
- Do not convert “interested” into purchase intent. A proposal or site-assessment request is the stronger signal.
