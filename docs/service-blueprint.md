# FacadeOps service blueprint

## Service promise

Help a property manager understand an exterior condition, retain the evidence, and decide the next appropriate action. The promise is a better-organized decision record, not an automatic diagnosis or a claim that drones are always the safest or cheapest method.

## Delivery stages

| Stage | Customer-visible activity | Behind-the-scenes activity | Gate | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Enquiry | Manager describes the building and decision required | Conflict, location, building-use, and feasibility screen | FacadeOps can describe the scope without implying a regulated conclusion | Discovery record |
| 2. Site assessment | Walk-around and review of existing records | Identify access, people, neighboring property, restricted areas, and likely capture methods | Site owner/manager authority verified | Site-assessment note |
| 3. Scope | Manager approves specific elevations and questions | Qualified technical partner defines evidence needs; operator assesses aircraft and airspace feasibility | Written scope, responsibilities, exclusions, and preliminary permissions | Inspection plan and quotation |
| 4. Authorization | Manager receives schedule and notice plan | Verify operator, pilot, aircraft, airspace, site, insurance, weather, privacy, and safety requirements | Every mandatory item recorded as verified | Go/no-go record |
| 5. Capture | Property receives planned disruption/notice information | Authorized operator captures the minimum required imagery; ground team controls the work area | Stop-work authority remains active | Controlled evidence set |
| 6. Review | Manager can answer clarification questions | Qualified reviewer classifies visible conditions and limitations; no unsupported diagnosis | Technical review complete | Draft record |
| 7. Delivery | Manager reviews evidence and next actions | Quality and privacy review; retention settings applied | Customer accepts report limitations | Inspection record and action list |
| 8. Follow-up | Manager updates action status | Evidence and completed work are linked to the same building history | Changes are attributable and dated | Recurring maintenance record |

## Responsibility model

- **Property manager:** confirms authority, building context, occupants, access, prior work, and required notices.
- **FacadeOps:** coordinates scope, evidence structure, privacy controls, record quality, and customer communication.
- **Authorized operator:** owns flight legality, aircraft readiness, pilot competence, flight plan, weather decision, and aviation records.
- **Qualified technical partner:** defines evidence requirements, reviews observations, distinguishes visible condition from diagnosis, and recommends when specialist investigation is required.
- **Contractor:** separately scopes and executes cleaning or repair. It must not silently convert an observation into a repair instruction.

## Stop conditions

Stop or reschedule when authorization is incomplete, the site has not been controlled, weather exceeds the operator's limits, privacy boundaries cannot be maintained, the requested conclusion exceeds the available competence, or the customer asks for a diagnosis unsupported by the evidence.

## Handoff quality standard

Every finding must include a stable identifier, observation date, facade/elevation, location description, evidence reference, observation, uncertainty or limitation, reviewer role, recommended next action, urgency rationale, and status. Words such as “structural,” “safe,” “compliant,” or “certified” require an explicitly qualified and accountable source.
