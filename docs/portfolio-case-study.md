# FacadeOps portfolio case study outline

## Framing

FacadeOps explores whether property managers in Luanda need a better way to turn difficult-to-access exterior evidence into a recurring maintenance record. It is an early-stage service-design and operational-research project, not a launched company.

## Portfolio narrative

1. **Observed question:** How do property managers decide what to inspect, maintain, or repair when evidence is expensive to collect and scattered across providers?
2. **Initial hypothesis:** A coordinated evidence and record service may be more useful than selling drone capture alone.
3. **Market correction:** Local research found existing drone inspection, drone washing, and facilities-management providers. The differentiation hypothesis shifted toward structured recurring records, independent coordination, and qualified interpretation.
4. **Safety and regulatory constraint:** The design treats authorization, competence, privacy, insurance, and site control as prerequisites rather than marketing footnotes.
5. **Prototype:** A service blueprint, synthetic inspection record, bilingual research instruments, pricing model, and validation-stage concept site.
6. **Research plan:** Ten property managers, three maintenance specialists, and two operator/regulatory/insurance specialists.
7. **Decision rule:** Proceed only with evidence of repeated demand, proposal requests, and viable accountable partners.

## Evidence to publish later

- Redacted synthesis of interviews and contradictions.
- Exact changes made to the service after research.
- Synthetic report screenshots, never customer building imagery without written permission.
- What remained unverified and why.
- A clear stop or continue decision after the 30-day validation period.

## Claims policy

Use “designed,” “researched,” “prototyped,” or “validated with N participants” only when supported. Do not use “launched,” “safer,” “faster,” “cheaper,” “certified,” or “first in Angola” without attributable evidence.
