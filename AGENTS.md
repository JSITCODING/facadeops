# FacadeOps workspace rules

FacadeOps is in validation. Treat every public or customer-facing claim as unverified unless a dated source or accountable partner record supports it.

## Boundaries

- Do not claim that FacadeOps is operating, licensed, certified, insured, safer, cheaper, faster, or first.
- Do not plan or conduct flights, buy equipment, contact customers, or submit regulatory applications without explicit user authorization.
- Keep identity documents, operator credentials, insurance documents, customer contact data, addresses, access details, raw interview responses, and building imagery outside Git.
- Use synthetic buildings and findings in repository examples.
- Do not treat imagery as an engineering diagnosis. Preserve the role of qualified technical reviewers.
- Keep the site labeled `Validation stage` until every applicable publication claim is reviewed.

## Development

- Inspect `git status` before editing and preserve unrelated work.
- Use English and Portuguese for public-facing content.
- Run privacy and secret scans before any push or visibility change.
- Do not create a remote, push, deploy, publish, or change visibility without explicit approval.
